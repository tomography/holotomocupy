Credits
=======


If you use holotomocupy, please cite the bilinear Hessian paper
:cite:`Nikitin:26`, which describes the joint object/probe/position solver
this package implements:

    Viktor Nikitin et al., "Scalable joint X-ray nano-holotomography
    reconstruction with the bilinear Hessian method", *Optica* **13**\ (9),
    1814-1826 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.603920

.. code-block:: bibtex

    @article{nikitin2026scalable,
      title={Scalable joint X-ray nano-holotomography reconstruction with the bilinear Hessian method},
      author={Nikitin, Viktor and Cloetens, Peter and Wendt, Herwig and Romanenko, Alexey and Laugros, Alfred and Sokolov, Andrey and Nguyen, Chi Thang and Zhang, Yuxin and Bosch, Carles and Schaefer, Andreas and others},
      journal={Optica},
      volume={13},
      number={9},
      pages={1814--1826},
      year={2026},
      publisher={Optica Publishing Group},
      doi={10.1364/OPTICA.603920}
    }
